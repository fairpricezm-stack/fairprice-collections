Terms & Conditions

Please read the following terms carefully before placing an order with FairPrice Collections.

FairPrice Collections — Quality. Fair Prices. Reliable Service.

1. Product Information & Customer Confirmation

Customers are advised to carefully review the product description, specifications, condition, photos, and any disclosed defects or limitations before making a purchase. If you require additional information, photos, or clarification about a product, please contact FairPrice Collections before making payment. By proceeding with a purchase, the customer confirms that they have reviewed the information provided and understand and accept the disclosed condition and specifications of the product.

2. Pre-Order Discount and Reservation

Pre-order/reservation items qualify for at least a 7% discount off the original selling price, unless otherwise stated. Customers are encouraged to confirm product availability, price, and the applicable discount before making payment.

3. Payment & Order Confirmation

Payments should only be made using the official payment details provided by FairPrice Collections. After making payment, customers must send proof of payment through the official FairPrice Collections WhatsApp Business platform or the provided official email address for verification. An order is considered confirmed only after payment has been successfully received and verified by FairPrice Collections.

4. Returns, Refunds & Warranty

Customers should inspect items promptly after delivery or collection and report any concerns within 48 hours, with proof of purchase and clear details of the issue. Return or refund requests made after 48 hours will generally not be accepted for a change of mind, normal wear and tear, misuse, accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs, or software changes made by the customer.

Where an item is faulty, unsuitable, materially different from its description, or has an undisclosed defect, FairPrice Collections will assess the matter and provide an appropriate remedy in line with applicable law. This policy does not limit customers’ legal rights.

5. Order Changes & Cancellations

Customers are advised to carefully confirm their product selection, specifications, condition, and price before making payment. Any request to change or cancel an order should be communicated as soon as possible. Changes or cancellations will depend on the status of the order, including whether the item has already been ordered, processed, shipped, or dispatched.

6. Pre-Order Processing & Estimated Arrival

Customers will be provided with an estimated arrival or delivery period for pre-order items. Delivery timelines are estimates and may occasionally be affected by shipping, customs clearance, courier delays, or other circumstances beyond our direct control. Where possible, customers will be informed of significant delays or changes affecting their orders.

7. Delivery Across Zambia

FairPrice Collections is based in Kitwe and delivers countrywide across Zambia. Applicable delivery or courier charges will be communicated before dispatch. Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact and delivery information to help ensure successful delivery.

8. Our Commitment to You

At FairPrice Collections, we are committed to providing quality products, transparent product information, fair prices, reliable service, and a convenient online shopping experience. We value the trust of our customers and aim to make every purchase as simple, transparent, and reliable as possible.
